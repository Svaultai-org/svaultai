SVaultAI — The Vault Your Family Can Actually Open

SVaultAI is an encrypted vault for the digital records that matter to you — logins, documents, photos, memories and supported asset records — with optional inheritance access planning built in. Pair a beneficiary with a code and prepare the vault credentials you want them to receive. A beneficiary can request access; you can approve or decline, or the prepared credentials can become available after the request's waiting period if you do not respond.

Inheritance: digital access planning

Pair a beneficiary with a code, then prepare an encrypted package containing the vault name and PIN you choose to share. A beneficiary must request access before the waiting period starts. Approve to make that package available; decline to stop that pending release. If you do not respond before the request's waiting period expires, your beneficiary can claim and reveal the prepared credentials. This is a digital access feature, not a legal will or a substitute for estate advice.

Ask the AI chat to find, save, retrieve and manage content you authorize inside your vault — "where's my passport scan?" — in everyday language. Client-side encryption keeps protected vault content under your control.
