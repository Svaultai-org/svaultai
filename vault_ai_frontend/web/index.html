Beneficiary access

Prepare encrypted vault credentials for a trusted person. Stay in control with requests, approval and decline, with a countdown for an unanswered request.

Prepare access for someone you trust.

  1. 1. Pair and prepare

    Connect by code and prepare encrypted vault credentials for your beneficiary.

  2. 2. Request access

    The beneficiary can request access if they believe the connected vault owner has died.

  3. 3. Approve or decline

    The owner signs in. Approval releases credentials immediately; declining clears the request and countdown.

  4. 4. An unanswered request

    After the countdown, normally 30 days, the beneficiary can claim and reveal the prepared credentials.

The owner signs in to check requests in the app. SVaultAI does not verify death. Prepared credentials can open the associated vault; this is not selective-file access, a legal will or an automatic transfer of assets.
