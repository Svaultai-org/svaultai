Features / Digital inheritance
The future of your Digital Vault

Prepare vault access for someone you trust.

A physical vault can protect important records for the future. SVaultAI brings that purpose into digital form with beneficiary pairing and an owner-prepared encrypted package containing a vault name and PIN for controlled release.

How the beneficiary waiting period works

Pair → prepare credentials → request → review → release or claim.

  1. Pair and prepare. Pair with someone you trust and prepare an encrypted package containing the vault name and PIN you choose to share. These credentials can unlock that vault; this is not a per-file sharing permission.
  2. The beneficiary requests access. After credentials are prepared, select Request access in Inheritance. This starts the waiting period, normally 30 days. The countdown shown in the app gives that request’s actual deadline.
  3. The owner reviews the request. An in-app notification tells the owner about the request. Open Notifications and Inheritance, then select Approve now or Reject request. Check in the app rather than relying on email or SMS alerts.
  4. Either side can stop a pending request. The beneficiary selects Cancel request; the owner selects Reject request. Either action clears that request’s countdown. The beneficiary can request again later.
  5. Approval or expiry enables access. Owner approval makes the prepared package available. If the owner does not respond before the countdown expires, the beneficiary can select Claim and reveal. Expiry does not automatically transfer assets or legal ownership.

A concrete example

You keep important family documents in a vault and prepare its vault name and PIN for your paired beneficiary. They request access; after approval or the request’s waiting period, they can reveal the prepared credentials and use them to sign in. The package is credentials, not a separate copy of selected documents.

This does not transfer ownership of a bank account, physical property or cryptocurrency. Legal authority, third-party account rules and wallet requirements still apply.

Not a recovery shortcut

Digital inheritance does not weaken the PIN model or create a general support backdoor. Vault owners remain responsible for their PIN, devices, recovery materials, and beneficiary choices.

Plan carefully

Only prepare credentials that are appropriate for the intended beneficiary, and review your arrangement when your circumstances change. Digital inheritance tools are not a legal will, legal estate advice or a guarantee of automatic physical-asset ownership or token transfer.

Keep important digital records organized, and use the digital inheritance checklist to think through access and responsibilities.
