Features / Digital inheritance
The future of your Digital Vault

Prepare selected digital information for a trusted beneficiary.

A physical vault can protect important records for the future. SVaultAI brings that purpose into digital form with beneficiary pairing and owner-prepared information for controlled release.

Owner-controlled preparation

Choose a beneficiary

Pair a trusted person through the supported beneficiary workflow.

Select what matters

Inheritance is based on information the owner deliberately prepares rather than automatic access to the entire vault.

Controlled release

Release state, pairing, and authorization checks are part of the protected workflow.

Not a recovery shortcut

Digital inheritance does not weaken the PIN model or create a general support backdoor. Vault owners remain responsible for their PIN, devices, recovery materials, and beneficiary choices.

Plan carefully

Only prepare records that are appropriate for the intended beneficiary, and review the selection when your circumstances change. Digital inheritance tools are not a legal will, legal estate advice or a guarantee of automatic physical-asset ownership or token transfer.

Keep important digital records organized, and use the digital inheritance checklist to think through access and responsibilities.
