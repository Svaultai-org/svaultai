What can you keep in a digital vault?

SVaultAI brings personal storage, retrieval and supported asset tools into one app. Choose the categories you need, and review each feature’s privacy and access requirements.

Files, documents and photos

Save important documents, private photos, videos and supported files. Retrieve a passport scan, receipt or family photo when you need it.

Logins

Keep account details in your vault. Find a named login, review it, update it or remove information you no longer need.

Memories

Preserve personal notes and details alongside your files. Use the memory timeline or chat to find supported saved information.

Digital assets and crypto

Use supported non-custodial cryptocurrency wallet tools, PAXG Digital Gold and Ethereum KAG Digital Silver. View balances, receive, review sends and see transaction history on supported networks.

AI chat

Ask about content you authorize in everyday language, such as “find my passport scan.” AI and document features may process authorized content on servers and with service providers.

Beneficiary access planning

Pair a trusted beneficiary and prepare credentials for future access. Review an access request, or let the request’s waiting period run before prepared access becomes claimable.

Digital assets belong in the picture

Crypto is a supported category inside SVaultAI’s Assets area. Digital Gold supports PAXG on Ethereum Mainnet; Digital Silver supports the KMS Labs KAG ERC-20 token on Ethereum Mainnet.

Wallet sending requires your approval. SVaultAI does not hold your funds or provide trading, brokerage or fiat purchases. Supported tokens, networks, plan eligibility, issuer terms and country restrictions determine what is available.

Understand access before you rely on it

Encryption and AI

Protected files and records are encrypted while stored. Your vault name and PIN are required to open your account; support cannot recover them if forgotten. AI and document features may process the content you authorize on servers and with service providers.

Read the privacy policy and security explanation.

Beneficiary access

Pair beneficiary → prepare credentials → review a request or wait for release. A request starts its waiting period. Approval makes the prepared package available; declining stops that pending release. If you do not respond before the period expires, the beneficiary can claim the prepared credentials.

This feature does not replace a legal will or transfer legal ownership. Read the complete access flow.

Digital vault questions

What is a digital vault?

A digital vault is a place to organize and protect important digital information. SVaultAI combines encrypted storage for supported personal content with AI retrieval, optional beneficiary access planning and supported digital-asset tools. Read the encrypted-vault guide.

How is it different from a password manager?

Logins are one part of SVaultAI. Files, private photos and videos, memories and supported digital assets also have dedicated features. Compare the two approaches.

Is the digital vault free?

The base vault is free to start. Optional paid storage plans are available, and cryptocurrency wallet features require an eligible paid storage plan. Check the current plan details in the app before upgrading.

Can I use it on the web and mobile?

SVaultAI is available on the web, iPhone, iPad and Android. Feature availability can depend on your current release, platform, plan and supported network.

Can support recover my vault name or PIN?

No. Keep both safe, and test your chosen access arrangements before relying on them. Optional beneficiary planning must be prepared in advance.
