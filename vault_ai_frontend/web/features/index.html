Assets: supported digital holdings, under your control

Cryptocurrency is one category inside Assets, alongside Digital Gold and Digital Silver. Supported wallet flows show live on-chain balances, receive addresses, send review and transaction history. Keys are encrypted, and sending requires your local approval. SVaultAI does not hold your funds or provide a trading, brokerage or fiat-purchase service.

Cryptocurrency

Use supported crypto assets and networks shown in the app. Check the exact asset and network before receiving or sending; availability can differ by network and deployment.

Digital Gold: PAXG

Manage the supported PAX Gold ERC-20 token on Ethereum Mainnet using your existing Ethereum wallet. Issuer eligibility, transfer restrictions and terms apply.

Digital Silver: KAG

Manage the supported KMS Labs KAG ERC-20 token on Ethereum Mainnet, not native Kinesis-network KAG. Issuer terms and eligibility apply; token holding is not a claim of direct ownership of silver bars.

Ethereum token transfers need ETH for network gas. Issuers can restrict or pause transfers, and third-party purchase or redemption requirements are separate from SVaultAI wallet support. Review PAX Gold terms and KMS Labs terms before use. Availability is not promised in every country.

Tokenized real estate, diamonds and gemstones, artwork, watches and collectibles, vehicles and equipment, inventory and supply chain, and securities and equities are marked Coming soon. They are not currently connected holdings and cannot be received or sent through those categories.

Concierge: free checks for your saved passwords

Opt in while your vault is unlocked to check eligible saved passwords against Have I Been Pwned Pwned Passwords, then review local weakness and reuse findings. Only a five-character password-hash prefix is sent for the exposure lookup; the complete password and hash stay on your device.

Findings help you identify which login to update. Change a compromised password on the original service, then update the saved record in SVaultAI. A no-match result means no known match in that source, not a guarantee that the password is safe.

No paid provider subscription is required for these password checks. Email checks, background email monitoring and stealer-log checks are not enabled. Private-file exposure scanning and comprehensive dark-web monitoring are not supported.

Common questions

Is SVaultAI a password manager?

Logins are one part of the Digital Vault. SVaultAI also protects supported files, private photos and videos, memories and inheritance information, with separate Assets and Concierge tools.

Can I put physical belongings in SVaultAI?

Store their supported digital records, such as a photograph, receipt or document. Storing a record does not transfer a physical item's ownership. Only verified supported blockchain tokens appear as live Assets.

Does Concierge monitor everything automatically?

No. The current free release offers opt-in saved-password checks while unlocked. It does not run full email, file or dark-web monitoring in the background.

Can support recover my PIN or move my funds?

Support cannot recover a forgotten vault name or PIN. Wallet sending requires your approval; keep your credentials and device access secure.

Available on web, iPhone, iPad, and Android

Use the same supported SVaultAI account across platforms. Your vault name and PIN are required to sign in. Feature availability depends on the app release, your plan, platform, network and issuer; use the capabilities shown in your current app.
