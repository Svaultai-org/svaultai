One part of your Digital Vault

Private logins, alongside the rest of your digital life.

SVaultAI is a digital vault, not simply a password manager. Keep named login records beside your private files and memories, then retrieve the correct username and password when you need them.

Create, save, retrieve, and delete

  1. Create a login draft. Give the record a recognizable service name and review the username and password before saving.
  2. Save it securely. The record is placed in your protected vault only after you confirm.
  3. Retrieve the right login. Ask for a saved service by name or browse the Logins area.
  4. Edit or delete it. Update a record when credentials change, or remove it when you no longer need it.

Designed for deliberate control

SVaultAI keeps drafts separate from saved records, requires confirmation before storage, and provides explicit edit and delete controls. Your vault name and PIN protect access; SVaultAI cannot recover a forgotten PIN under the current security model.

Review password exposure with Concierge

While unlocked, opt in to free checks for known saved-password exposure and local weakness or reuse. Concierge sends only a five-character password-hash prefix to Pwned Passwords, not the complete password or hash. Results are limited to known provider records; a no-match is not a guarantee of safety.

Change affected credentials on the original service before updating the saved login. Read what Concierge checks and what it does not monitor.
